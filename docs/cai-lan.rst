随笔七十二 Chua Lam
============================

Chua Lam is a man who has truly lived life on his own terms. From his early days in Singapore to his success in Hong Kong’s film industry, his emergence as a cultural tastemaker, and his enduring legacy as a writer and food critic, his life epitomizes the idea of celebrating life’s pleasures. Through his work, he encourages others to approach life with curiosity, humor, and an open heart. His philosophy can be summarized as: “Don’t take life too seriously—enjoy it.”

Chua Lam, one of Asia's most renowned food critics and columnists to make a mark on Hong Kong's entertainment and culinary scene, died on Wednesday at the age of 83.

His death was announced on Chua's social media account on Friday afternoon, with the post saying that he died peacefully at the Hong Kong Sanatorium and Hospital in the company of his family and friends.

"In accordance with Chua's wishes, no ceremony will be held, and his body was cremated to avoid disturbing his relatives and friends," the post said.

Chua Lam is a prominent figure in the Asian culinary world, known for his expertise as a food critic, columnist, and gastronomic influencer. With a career spanning decades, Chua Lam has established himself as one of the most revered and influential voices in the realm of food and dining.

His deep knowledge of Asian cuisine, coupled with a discerning palate and an engaging writing style, has garnered him a wide readership and a dedicated following. Chua Lam's contributions to the culinary landscape extend beyond his writings; he is also celebrated for his television appearances, public speaking engagements, and advocacy for Asian culinary traditions.

Chua Lam's comprehensive understanding of food culture, coupled with his ability to communicate the nuances of taste, texture, and technique, has made him an authority in the field. His work has not only celebrated the richness of Asian culinary heritage but has also influenced and shaped the way in which people perceive and appreciate food.

Throughout his career, Chua Lam has continuously offered insights, recommendations, and critiques that have guided and inspired both aspiring chefs and enthusiastic diners, earning him a well-deserved reputation as an esteemed and influential figure in the world of gastronomy.
